Everything a private time tracker needs

And nothing it does not. No feeds, no social, no upsell.

One tap to start

The running session is written to the database immediately, so closing the app, locking the screen or a reboot never loses it.

Finish with a summary

Confirm the end time, break, rate, work type and note before saving. Everything is editable in one screen.

Charts that state numbers

Values are drawn on the bars and every data point is also listed underneath, so a month of work is readable at a glance.

Calendar with day controls

Long-press any day to record hours for it, mark it as a rest day, or give it its own target. Every choice is remembered.

Your own background

Pick a colour or your own picture, blur it from 0% to 100%, and let the app take its accent from the image.

Schedule and targets

Set a weekly hours target and your rest days; the dashboard and statistics show progress against them.

Your data, portable

Export CSV for a spreadsheet or a full JSON backup, and import it back with automatic duplicate detection.

English, 日本語, 中文

Switch language inside the app, or from the system "App language" screen on Android 13 and newer.

Designed to be read in three seconds

Big numbers, generous space, and colour used only where it means something.

Nothing leaves your phone

Workly asks for no permissions at all. There is no internet permission in the manifest, which means the app is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

  • No account, no sign-in, no email
  • No cloud sync and no server of any kind
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers
  • Backups are plain files you choose where to save
